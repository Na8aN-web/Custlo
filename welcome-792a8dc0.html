Since the start of 2025, I've had a burning concern for the future of customer facing roles in Africa, and how much opportunity for individuals to maximize their careers is still being left on the table.

So, my goal is simple, to help and support focused customer professionals feeling stuck to have clarity and precision on what they want and how to go get it.

But who do I think I am?

My name is George Kaduru, and I'm a passionate lover of people and technology (mostly people, lol). I've been in Customer Support and Experience for over 5 years, and this has seen me serve clients across the globe in many customer facing roles.

There is so much Customer Talent in Africa, but that alone is not enough. To truly thrive, you must also be strategic and intentional to seek and make the most of opportunities, both home and abroad. This is what Custlo is about.

We are a community of customer loving people who know what we want and go get it. We foster togetherness in the hustle of our careers, share unique experiences with life lessons and most of all, we are a people who really love what we do, and are pretty good at it too.

So, if you resonate with any of this, then I would really love to have you on this journey with us.
